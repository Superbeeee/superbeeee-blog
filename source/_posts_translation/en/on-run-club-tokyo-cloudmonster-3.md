Hi! I'm Eason — coming up on three years of running and a runner who loves sharing On Running info. 🏃

After finishing the Tokyo Marathon earlier this month, I browsed On's Japanese site to see what offline events were on. With time on hand, I signed up for this one — a session at the Ginza flagship.

This is my sixth piece on Sports Note: an unboxing of the flagship store and the ORC event!

## A Quick Unboxing of the Ginza Flagship ✨

I actually visited the store earlier in the afternoon. There was a queue to enter — about a 30-minute wait, less than I expected.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/03-flagship-front.jpg" alt="Flagship storefront">

<figure>
<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/04-queue-sign.jpg" alt="Queue start sign">
<figcaption>The little sign marking the queue start</figcaption>
</figure>

The moment you step in, On's signature slogan **"Dream On."** stares back at you. As a fan, this is genuine excitement-mode. Camera up, photo down. <3

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/05-dream-on.jpg" alt="Dream On slogan wall">

The first floor is all shoes. The second floor has shoes plus apparel — but business has been so good that lots of pieces were out of stock in many sizes. For backpacks and accessories, you have to actively ask staff what's in inventory; not everything is on display.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/06-store-1f.jpg" alt="First floor shoes section">

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/07-store-2f.jpg" alt="Second floor apparel section">

I picked a slightly off day — a new apparel drop was scheduled for the next day, so I missed out a bit.

I tried on a tennis-line top. The fitting room is large, but the industrial-cement wall surface is a bit rough on the skin — careful when you turn around. XD

<figure>
<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/08-shopping.jpg" alt="First-time flagship shopping">
<figcaption>First-time flagship shopping ~</figcaption>
</figure>

In the end I bought an On shoe bag. If you've picked items but want to keep browsing, you can hand them to staff at the counter for a numbered ticket — settle everything together when you check out. Convenient.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/09-shoe-bag.jpg" alt="On shoe bag">

## Tokyo On Run Club Event — Trial Run with On Cloudmonster 3 ✨

I'd already spotted On Cloudmonster 3 (hereafter "Monster 3") at the Tokyo Marathon Expo. Japan, capitalizing on the Tokyo Marathon hype, was the first market to launch it. I'd assumed the event would be a generic city run; only on arrival did I realize it was a proper trial-run session. Sweet.

Arriving in the evening, the queue was still out the door — I just showed staff my registration screenshot and walked straight in. Earlier in the afternoon I'd noticed the cryptic B1 Run Club on the floor map and that it didn't seem open to the public. Tonight's ORC event was my chance to peek behind the curtain. ^0^

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/10-orc-night-queue.jpg" alt="Evening queue at the store">

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/11-b1-entrance.jpg" alt="B1 entrance">

The B1 isn't as large as the upper floors — most of it is staff space. The open area held the night's main character, **On Cloudmonster 3**. Center: event space. Sides: changing rooms, lockers, and a small fitness corner. The lockers support custom passcodes (thoughtful), but I went with the default since I was scared of forgetting it ^^a. Definitely worth using if you join an in-person event.

<figure>
<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/12-locker.jpg" alt="Convenient locker">
<figcaption>Convenient lockers!</figcaption>
</figure>

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/13-cloudmonster-3.jpg" alt="On Cloudmonster 3">

First time on the Monster 3 — the improvement over the previous generation is significant. When I tried the Monster 2, the heel stability was lacking; uneven ground or any small bump and I'd risk a roll. This time the wrap and support are noticeably better. I could not wait to actually run in them.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/14-on-foot.jpg" alt="On foot">

A staff member walked us through the shoe and explained that we'd do a relay near the Imperial Palace area. Then everyone counted off 1-2-3-4 around the room — same number = same team.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/15-event-brief.jpg" alt="Event briefing">

During warm-up I heard the Japanese-style warm-up call-and-response for the first time — felt like attending PE class here. Genuinely fresh experience.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/16-warmup.jpg" alt="Warm-up">

After we set off, I met Tsugumi-san, a runner from Nara. She was in Tokyo on a business trip, and she'd recently been doing Hyrox events with friends. Coincidence: Taipei's Hyrox was just last week, and she'd just finished one in Korea.

The relay start happened to be right by the Tokyo Marathon finish.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/17-relay-start.jpg" alt="Relay start">

Just-finished-Tokyo legs meant I couldn't run fast — but watching everyone relay in the cold was still electric. I helped my team film some clips. Not for first place; just because of the small fate that brought us together to run. Genuinely fun. On the way back, I got too into a conversation, didn't see a raised section in the floor, and almost ate it — quick reflexes saved me from a real spill.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/18-running.jpg" alt="Running">

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/19-relay-team.jpg" alt="Relay team">

By the time the event wrapped, the store had closed. We headed back to B1. Staff summarized the night and announced that buying the Monster 3 came with a Tokyo-exclusive towel. Tempting — never seen this item as a fan — but realistically I already have plenty of shoes (QQ), so I held the line. Rational consumption mode. XD

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/20-back-at-store.jpg" alt="Back at the store">

Earlier, before the event started, I'd chatted with one of the managers and mentioned I'd just finished Tokyo Marathon and dropped by for the event. Before I left, that same manager came over with an On towel and said:

> "This is a gift to celebrate finishing Tokyo Marathon. Congratulations to you!"

In the moment I was genuinely moved. Thanks to that gentleman, and to everyone who put on this event — really good memories.

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/21-tokyo-towel.jpg" alt="Tokyo towel gift">

One last thing — on the way out, staff showed me a QR code to give feedback on the event. After completing it, I got an On jump rope and a Monster 3 introduction card. The card listed designated check-in spots; collect enough and you're entered into a Monster 3 raffle. Show the card before end-April and you can skip the queue and walk straight into the store. A VIP pass, basically. ^0^

<img src="/post/2026/03/on-run-club-tokyo-cloudmonster-3/22-vip-card.jpg" alt="VIP card">
